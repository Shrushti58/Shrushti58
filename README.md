## About

I build things that solve problems people actually have — mostly in places where good software rarely reaches. Right now that means GramVartha, a platform trying to close the gap between Gram Panchayats and the citizens they serve, powered by a bit of AI to sort real complaints from noise and point people toward government schemes they didn't know existed.

Computer Engineering student at D. Y. Patil College of Engineering, Akurdi (SPPU) — CGPA 9.46/10, previously a Diploma topper at 93.60%. I write MERN and React Native for a living (well, almost), freelance on the side for small businesses that just need a clean website and someone who won't overcomplicate it, and spend a surprising amount of time helping my college pass audits as IQAC Coordinator.

**GramVartha** — React Native (Expo), Node.js, MongoDB, Gemini API · Oct 2025 – March 2026 · Live
The project I care about most. Villages get flooded with fake or duplicate complaints just as often as real ones, so I built a verification pipeline that runs uploaded photos through Google Vision and Gemini together, scoring how genuine an issue actually looks before an official ever sees it. Paired that with a scheme-finding assistant that talks like a person, not a government form, and an offline-first notice system where one QR scan is all a village needs — no signal, no problem.
[View on GitHub](https://github.com/Shrushti58/GramVartha)

**eBachat Gat** — MERN Stack · Jan 2025 – Apr 2025 · Live
My first real project, and it shows in the best way — I learned more shipping this than in any course. It digitizes Bachat Gats, the community savings groups that keep a lot of Indian households afloat, with a proper approval chain (President signs off, Treasurer pays out), auto-generated PDF statements so nobody's doing math by hand anymore, and Razorpay handling the money so it doesn't have to.
[View on GitHub](https://github.com/Shrushti58/eBachatGat)

**CivicConnect** — MERN Stack · Sept 2025 – Nov 2025
Built with a team for Smart India Hackathon — a way for citizens to flag potholes, broken streetlights, and overflowing bins without it turning into the same complaint filed five times over. Geolocation tagging, photo uploads, and a bit of logic to catch duplicates before they clutter an official's dashboard. Landed in the top two projects internally.
[View on GitHub](https://github.com/adityaborhade18/SIH_Project)
